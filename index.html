Gate reviews are conducted by the ExCo Programme Office with CFO and CIO sign-off required for Tier 1 programmes. Gate failure (unresolved critical issues at review) triggers a mandatory programme pause and remediation plan within 15 business days.

Unapproved scope changes discovered in governance reviews are logged as change control breaches and require retrospective approval.

Programme risks are potential future events that could adversely affect delivery, benefits, budget, or schedule. Unlike issues (which have already materialised), risks are forward-looking. All risks are scored by likelihood (Low / Medium / High) and impact (Low / Medium / High) using the Group ERM matrix (High/High = Critical; Medium/High and High/Medium = High; ExCo may override upward for regulatory, patient-safety or Board-gate exposure; the 7 High and 5 Critical risks are itemised, the 6 Medium risks are not). Critical risks are escalated to the ExCo Programme Office and reported to the Board ARC quarterly. Risk appetite for transformation programmes is “moderate” — accept medium risks with mitigation; escalate all high-impact risks.
